How to Take Care of Plants

  • Water: Water plants regularly.
  • Sunlight:Provide Suitable sunlight.
  • Soil:Use good-quality soil.
  • Fertilizer:Add fertilizer when needed.
  • Cleaning:Remove dry leaves.

Important Tip!

Every plant needs proper care, water and sunlight to grow healhty and strong.
